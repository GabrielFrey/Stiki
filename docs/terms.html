The app

Stiki lets you make stickers from photos and video on your device and export them. The free plan includes unlimited static stickers, three packs, every export destination, and no watermark. Stiki Pro unlocks unlimited packs, animated stickers, advanced outlines and filters, and optional iCloud sync.

Purchases and subscriptions

Digital purchases are sold as in-app purchases through Apple:

  • Stiki Pro — One-Time (com.stiki.pro.lifetime): a non-consumable purchase. You pay once for that Apple ID (Family Sharing is enabled).
  • Stiki Pro — Yearly (com.stiki.pro.yearly): an auto-renewable subscription. Length: one year. Price: the amount shown in the paywall and on the App Store for your storefront currency. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, or from the paywall’s Manage subscription control.

There is no free trial and no weekly plan. Restore purchases on any device signed in with the same Apple ID. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Your content

You keep the rights to photos and stickers you create. You are responsible for having the rights to the media you import and for what you export to other apps. Stiki is not affiliated with WhatsApp, Telegram, or other third-party messengers; those names, if mentioned, describe file or pasteboard formats only.

No account

Stiki does not require a login. Optional iCloud sync uses your Apple ID through Apple’s CloudKit.

Disclaimer

Stiki is provided “as is.” On-device Vision cutout quality depends on the photo. Telegram animated WebM is not encoded on device in the current version; Stiki falls back to GIF or WebP and tells you.
